Can I change my team after Phase 1?

Yes, you can change your team composition after Phase 1, but certain limitations and penalty structures apply depending on the current protocol state.

Current Regulations (v3.2)

  • Phase 2 Transition: Teams are locked for the first 48 hours of Phase 2 to allow for initial neural sync.
  • Member Swapping: Permitted with a 5% consensus tax on accumulated data tokens.
  • Total Dissolution: Not available until Phase 4 (Stabilization).
info

Note: Changes made during the "Red Zone" (final 24 hours of any phase) will incur double latency penalties. It is highly recommended to finalize transitions during the "Clear Window" between Phase 1 and Phase 2.

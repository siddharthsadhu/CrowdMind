Can a team be changed after Phase 1?

Operations Phase Transition

No, teams are generally finalized at the conclusion of Phase 1: Discovery. Once the project moves into Phase 2: Execution, the structural roster is locked to ensure accountability and resource allocation stability.

Exception Criteria:

  • Major medical or logistical emergencies (requires Admin approval).
  • Consensual team dissolution (all 4+ members must sign off).
  • Platform-initiated realignment due to project cancellation.

To request a special exemption, navigate to the Administrative Hub and file a "Structural Change Request" (Form #77-B). Note that this may delay your milestone disbursement by up to 14 days.

# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in CrowdMind a welcoming experience for everyone.

Regardless of:

- Experience Level
- Education
- Nationality
- Gender
- Background

---

# Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- Be respectful
- Be constructive
- Be collaborative
- Be professional
- Be open to feedback

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment
- Personal Attacks
- Hate Speech
- Discrimination
- Threats
- Trolling

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers may:

- Warn
- Remove Content
- Restrict Access
- Remove Contributors

when necessary.

---

# Reporting

Report violations to project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

---

# Guiding Principle

Critique ideas.

Never attack people.
